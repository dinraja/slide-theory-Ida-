Algorithm explorer

Each number is that tile's Manhattan distance from its goal square. The gap counts as zero.

Cost breakdown

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

g = moves made: 0
h = total Manhattan distance: 0
f = g + h: 0

Why h is admissible: a tile needs at least one move per square of distance, and each move shifts one tile by one square, so h never overestimates. IDA* searches depth-first within successive f = g + h bounds. With this heuristic, its first solution is a shortest solution.

How to play

  1. Click or tap a tile beside the gap to slide it in. Arrow keys slide the tile on the opposite side of the arrow into the gap.
  2. Arrange tiles 1 to 15 in order with the gap in the bottom-right corner.
  3. The timer starts on your first move. Undo takes back a move and its count.
  4. Hint highlights a suggested tile. Solve runs IDA* and plays the answer back.
